Welcome to SpeechPoint

Use Chrome with your voice. Open sites, click, type, scroll and read — hands-free.

1Let SpeechPoint hear you

Chrome is asking for the microphone — click Allow.

2Open the SpeechPoint panel

Click the SpeechPoint icon in Chrome's toolbar, or press Option + S.

Tip: click the puzzle piece in the toolbar and pin SpeechPoint so it's always there.

3Just talk

  • “Go to YouTube” · “Watch funny cat videos” · “Pause”
  • “Click Sign in” · “The second result” · “Scroll down”
  • “Click search” then “Type pizza near me” · “Press enter”
  • “Start dictation” to write emails and messages by talking
  • “Show numbers” then a number, for anything without a name
  • “When I say homework, go to classroom.google.com” — your own shortcuts
  • “Help” anytime

SpeechPoint asks before pressing Send, Buy, Delete and similar buttons.
